## 💡 Actionable Insights & Recommendations
* **Address Geographic Imbalance:** North America and Europe account for over 71% of total revenue. Reallocate 15% of mature-market ad spends to Asia-Pacific and Latin America digital acquisition channels to mitigate regional cluster risk.
* **Deploy Enterprise Product Bundling:** Maximize hardware momentum by bundling low-velocity components (e.g., Dual-Arm Monitor Mounts) as integrated accessories with high-velocity anchors (e.g., 4K Ultra-HD Monitors) to boost asset turnover.
* **Plug Revenue Leakage:** Tighten discount triggers inside the CRM to preserve gross margin percentages, as minor transactional discounts (5% to 10%) are frequently applied without driving a corresponding linear increase in item volume.
